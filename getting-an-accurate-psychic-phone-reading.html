Getting an Accurate Psychic Phone Reading

Home›Guest Articles›Numerology

Not everyone can go to a psychic in their local areas. Some people don’t want to because they live in a small town. Or maybe you live in a city and you often pass by several shop fronts advertising psychic services, but the things like crystal balls and magic wands make you think they’re not the most authentic people working in the field.

All of these reasons might contribute to your desire to find a psychic online. You can have a single consultation or develop a relationship with an authentic psychic. The best part is that you can find one with whom you can talk about anything you want, any time that you want. You won’t be limited by when you can drive to an office, and you probably won’t be limited by traditional business hours.

What Can You Learn from a Psychic Phone Reading from Cherry Sage?

Cherry Sage is an authentic psychic who offers psychic phone readings that use both her psychic abilities and various psychic tools such as tarot cards. If you want a numerology or tarot card reading, there’s no reason that you can’t get started right away. These tools can be used no matter who you are and where you are to give a reading of the forces that are currently acting on your life–from within and from all around you.

The use of clairaudient and clairvoyant powers can sometimes yield very strong and immediate results. Occasionally a client will ask something like “What is John thinking with regard to me right now?” and I will be able to enter a meditative state and see or hear a clear vision that will give that client the answers he or she needs.

Other times I am not able to access a vision immediately–but unlike some of the fraudulent “psychics” out there, I will never lie to a client. Sometimes some answers will elude you during a psychic phone reading and remain mysterious. We are not meant to know everything. One should not reasonably expect that a psychic reading can tell all just like no psychic reader can tell or see all. That is a false claim. I am all about the truth and relaying information just as I receive it during your psychic reading. I will tell you what you need to know and what is being given. Know that what you are not given in a reading is for a reason, and not necessarily a reflection of a psychic’s abilities, but rather a need for you to experience a certain revelation or unexpected event because there is a lesson in it. No psychic can prevent you from learning your lessons and to know certain things “before” it happens may impede that lesson and is the reason that information is not given no matter how many psychics you ask. By knowing certain information, you may think you are bypassing a specific difficulty you may be having. But, this is not really correct. No one bypasses a difficulty that is presented to you specifically for karmic correction. The experience is necessary for soul growth. So although your psychic reading can be packed with insight and future events can be gleaned, specific events occur AFTER a sequence of actions or decisions which cannot be altered. The timing of those events can and many times do change due to avoidance which delays the soul’s growth and instead of a lesson being behind you, it remains ahead of you, to be experienced, ready or not! But what is the sense in avoidance, it is one of man’s greatest challenges?
