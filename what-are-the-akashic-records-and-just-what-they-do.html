What Are the Akashic Records and What Do They Do?

Home›Blog›Featured Articles

What Are the Akashic Records?

The Akashic Records are described as the universal archive of every soul's experiences, a detailed record of your soul's journey from its beginning to the present day. They're sometimes compared to a vast library, or metaphorically called a "universal supercomputer" or the "mind of God."

At their core, the Akashic Records are an energetic record that exists in the ether. It sounds abstract, but much like the internet, it's a real source of information you can learn to access once you know how. Think of it as a filing system, an energetic library that holds every thought, feeling, event, and experience since the beginning of time. You don't necessarily need to understand why the Records exist, only that they can be accessed to help guide your present life. That energy isn't localized anywhere, it's everywhere.

How Are the Records Accessed?

Accessing the Akashic Records isn't something you can do casually from your normal, waking state of mind, though some especially sensitive or intuitive people can do it more easily than others. There are real limits on who can access the Records and why: a clear, pure intention matters. If someone's intent is harmful, access simply isn't granted. One useful comparison is the internet itself, every soul has its own "page," all interconnected with everyone else's.

What You Can Learn From Them

The Records can be explored in a number of directions. You might look into ancient civilizations, past ancestors, or other members of your soul family. You can also examine your current relationships and gain a clearer understanding of why you're connected to the people you are, including how many lifetimes you may have shared and in what roles. Someone who is your sibling in this life may have been a parent or child in another. At a soul level, you continually choose how you experience these relationships and what they mean.

Healing Through the Akashic Records

The most common reason people seek out the Akashic Records is for healing. Maybe you're facing a limitation in your life that other approaches haven't resolved. Sometimes conscious understanding alone doesn't explain why a particular struggle keeps showing up.

Working with the Akashic Records for healing works at a soul level. We're each here with a spiritual purpose, a reason for choosing this lifetime, and much of that purpose becomes clearer once you understand whether you're living up to your soul's potential. Accessing the Akashic Records is one more real tool for understanding why you're here.
