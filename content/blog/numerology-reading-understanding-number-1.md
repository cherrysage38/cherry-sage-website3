**What does the number 1 mean in numerology?**

In numerology, Number 1 represents new beginnings, leadership, independence, creativity and self-confidence. People with a 1 in their core numbers tend to be natural leaders with strong willpower and a strong sense of self. In balance, 1 brings inner strength and the drive to achieve; out of balance, it can tip into self-centeredness and egotism, or the opposite, dependency.

## What are the traits of a Number 1?

In numerology, Number 1 is considered a powerful and influential number, representing new beginnings, leadership, creativity, independence and self-confidence. It is often associated with ambition, achievement and success.

People with a Life Path number of 1 are said to be natural leaders with strong willpower, determination and a strong sense of self. They are independent thinkers and tend to be innovative, creative and original in their ideas and actions. They are often driven to succeed and may excel in fields such as entrepreneurship, management or creative work.

Number 1 is also associated with unity and oneness. As the first number, it is considered the source of all other numbers, and in this sense it can represent the individual's connection to universal consciousness, or a higher power.

Overall, Number 1 is a powerful and positive number, representing the potential for new beginnings, self-discovery and leadership. People with a Life Path of 1 are said to have the potential to achieve great success and fulfillment, but they may also need to watch their tendency toward self-centeredness and egotism.

## What does a balanced Number 1 look like?

**Number 1: Individuation, Independence, Attainment**

**Positive characteristics:** These traits tend to be obvious, although they can be hidden depending on other factors. Much inner strength, leadership capability, and strong personal needs and desires. A 1 feels it is necessary to follow their own convictions.

Number 1 is the emergence of the ego. 1s are usually concerned with themselves and their own needs. Depending on the intensity, this can be expressed in selfish ways, and when expressed negatively, a 1 can be overbearing in the desire to have what it wants.

## What does an out-of-balance Number 1 look like?

Just because 1s are self-centered (and represent the beginning stages of human existence) doesn't mean the opposite can't be expressed. Underbalanced 1s can struggle with dependency and may seem to be at everyone's beck and call. Go figure. This reminds us how energy sits on a continuum, from one extreme to the other. Where you are at any given moment has a lot to do with how evolved you are. Balance is the goal, and that lands us smack dab in the center, not at either extreme.

Some 1s, lacking a sense of balance, may be so dominating and egotistical that they become their own worst enemies. Sometimes they burst with overconfidence, making them impatient with others' advice and unable to listen and benefit from other people's opinions.
