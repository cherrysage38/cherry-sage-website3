**Why do psychics say different things?**

Because not every psychic is real, and the ones who aren't can only fabricate the details. Many fake psychics work from scripts, so the broad picture they give you sounds the same (he loves you, you're soul mates, it's just a matter of time), but the details vary greatly, because they don't know them and have no way of knowing. Calling one psychic after another to verify what you were told only leaves you more confused.

Getting real, accurate psychic predictions can be very elusive these days. You may have received a number of readings, and now you are wondering why psychics make different predictions or say different things. There are so many psychic venues where you can get a reading: psychic lines, psychic websites, bookstores, gypsy psychic houses and more. There are also several types of readers: clairvoyants, tarot card readers, psychics who read runes or tea leaves, and psychics who use other tools such as numerology and astrology.

## Does using tools mean a psychic isn't really psychic?

Let's get something clear. Psychics who use tools can very much be naturally psychic or spiritually gifted. In fact, they can be quite powerful in this capacity. When a person is using higher spiritual gifts to access information that would otherwise be hidden, using tools does not lessen their ability in the least, and could in fact be a powerful aid for them.

Some people use tools because they are learning. Sometimes a person with raw psychic ability who wants to become a professional psychic will start out by studying the Tarot. With time and training, they can gradually sharpen their skill to the point where they can offer their talent on a professional level. But becoming a psychic (or, as I prefer, a psychic spiritual counselor) means more than learning the meanings of tarot cards or studying other ancient tools. You have to have a gift to provide an authentic experience.

So whether a psychic divines the past, present or future with or without tools should not be your concern. What should concern you is whether you are dealing with an authentic and ethical intuitive in the first place.

Don't fool yourself. There are people with genuine psychic ability who have no scruples and will tell you things just to get you in their control and rob you of all your money. Do not assume that a gifted person, by virtue of having a spiritual gift, is from the divine, has blessed origins, or holds themselves to a high standard. Be very discerning and not so trusting, unless you have been referred by someone who has had a truly valuable and verifiable experience.

## Why do so many psychics tell you the same thing?

Psychics can say the same things, which may give you a secure feeling that what they are saying must be accurate. Wrong.

As a professional psychic counselor, I have found that when you call a psychic network with hundreds or thousands of readers, you will hear basically the same broad picture over and over, because you have encountered a scripted reading, not an authentic one. By scripted, I mean they have familiarized themselves with certain responses based on what you are asking.

You may not be aware that psychic hotlines used to be how most people got their psychic advice. These psychics were provided with scripts, manuals and handbooks: literal scripts for what to say about every aspect of life you can imagine. Thousands memorized these scripts and made them their own. So each fake psychic you call will give you a variation on a familiar theme. There is an arsenal of these readers out there, just waiting for you to call so they can confuse you.

The script tells you exactly what you are eager to hear: the person you are calling about loves you, you will get married one day, you are soul mates, it's just a matter of time. That's the broad picture. They cannot give you real details because a) they do not know the details and have no way of knowing, and b) you are likely filling in the details yourself.

## I called a psychic for clarity. Why am I so confused?

If you call a number of psychics and they are saying different things, the differences are in the details. You are now calling one after another to see if one verifies the other. The broad picture is the same, but the details are all different, and you end up more confused than when you set out to get clarity.

In a scripted reading, the reader is telling you what you want to hear, not what you need to hear. In an authentic, [real psychic reading](/psychic-reading), the psychic intuitive is accessing information on a spiritual level. People seem to forget this. Through them, you are getting guided information that originates from higher consciousness. You will hear what is necessary for you to know to go forward and reach balanced solutions in your life.

You are not meant to know everything you want to know, like whether you will marry someone you just met, when you will be living together, or how many kids you will have. You assume that's the next step because you were told they were your soul mate, so now you want all the details about the future. That, by the way, takes you off course, like following a false trail. Spiritual channels, which psychics are supposed to be, cannot guarantee your future. You will find yourself asking questions psychics don't have the answers to, and fake psychics will be happy to provide false answers. The details they can only fabricate. That's why they say different things.

Having a realistic idea of what to expect from a real reading will give you a much more valuable experience. You have to have the correct mindset to receive higher guidance. A scripted reading is fortune telling, an "entertainment" form of psychic reading that dominates the psychic industry today. The gypsy psychic scam takes the fake reading to a whole other level: it exists for the sole purpose of conning you out of every single dime, often until you have nothing left.

## Can a psychic really tell the future?

The work you do with a real psychic has more to do with revealing information about yourself and the path you are on, as it relates to your questions about others. A real psychic can feel and see a person's thoughts or feelings, and can divine the future based on the energy identified at the time of the reading.

I have found it rare that an "assumed" psychic can accurately tell the future. It is not a common gift, and no one can guarantee your future with another person. It is easy to get caught up in a fake psychic's lie and start believing a false idea that takes you on an odyssey and leaves you disillusioned and confused beyond belief.
