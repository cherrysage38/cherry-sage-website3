**Can a psychic really read you over the phone?**

Yes. When a psychic reads you, in person or over the phone, they are accessing your energy, the human energy field, and a gifted psychic can do that without being in your physical presence. A phone reading is just as accurate as an in-person reading for everyday questions about relationships, decisions and obstacles. The exceptions are very specific cases, like helping law enforcement with missing persons, where a psychic may need to be present or hold an object.

Over more than two decades, I have conducted well over 20,000 [phone psychic readings](/psychic-reading.html). That's right: my clients have found my readings detailed, accurate and sometimes amazing, right over the phone.

## How can a psychic read someone over the phone?

I am often asked how a psychic can give an accurate reading over the phone. My clients' testimonials speak volumes about how accurate a phone reading can be, but let me explain how it works.

When a psychic gives a reading, whether in person or over the phone, they are accessing energy: the human energy field. This energy usually cannot be seen physically, but it can be accessed psychically by gifted individuals with an advanced sixth sense. For a more scientific account of how auras, or the human energy field, can be measured with biofeedback devices, see the Spiritual Research Foundation. A gifted, practicing psychic has developed and sharpened their ability to sense energy in different ways, for example through clairvoyance, clairaudience, and empathically.

> "We perceive the gross or seen world through the five physical senses (i.e. smell, taste, sight, touch and sound), our mind (our feelings), and our intellect (decision making capacity). When it comes to the unseen world or the subtle world, we perceive it through the five subtle senses, the subtle mind and the subtle intellect – more popularly known as our sixth sense. When the sixth sense is developed or activated, it helps us to experience the subtle world or subtle dimension. This experience of the subtle world is also known as a 'spiritual experience'." (Source: Spiritual Research Foundation)

## Is a phone reading as accurate as an in-person reading?

An authentic experience with a real phone psychic will give you accurate information. Over the phone, the psychic uses the subtle senses of psychic intuition, together with the professional ability to understand the subtle cause and effect behind events in your life, to give you a spiritual reading that can empower you.

Have no doubt that a real psychic can access your auric energy without being in your physical presence. They can help you understand complex issues where you face obstacles, big or small, and give you valuable insights that shine a light on your path and help you make better choices.

## When does a psychic need to be there in person?

There are exceptions. Sometimes a psychic or medium needs to be in a person's physical presence, or to read an object, because the energy field of an object carries knowledge of its history. This is known as *psychometry*. It applies especially to missing persons, or to helping law enforcement solve crimes. Those readings go beyond what most people need for everyday relationships and obstacles, and in those cases it can be crucial for the psychic or medium to be present.

## Why choose a phone psychic reading?

A phone reading is an excellent way to get true, spiritually based answers and predictions, find solutions, and feel empowered in your life. You are not missing anything by having your reading over the phone. A professional psychic accesses the very same energy as if you were sitting in front of them, connects with you just by talking with you, and gives you an accurate reading.
