## What Is a Cancer Man Like?

The Cancer man is a private person who takes time to open up. He can be flirtatious and unpredictable, but underneath that he's sensitive and deeply devoted. His manner can come across as crabby or gruff at first, but that gruffness melts quickly into warmth, often followed by a sudden, completely infectious laugh. Few people appreciate a good laugh more than a Cancer man, even though his humor often comes from sharp, quietly perceptive observations about people.

## Old-Fashioned Romance, Grounded Nature

He is old-fashioned enough to properly court someone, and he can be one of the most romantic, dreamy partners you'll meet, while still keeping both feet on the ground. His moods may shift, but his core nature stays consistent. He has a deep respect for the past and often develops a real interest in antiques and heirlooms.

## Why He Retreats Into His Shell

On the surface, a Cancer man can seem tough and distant, but there's a genuinely caring nature underneath. When he feels too exposed, he withdraws, the "crab" retreating into its shell for protection, and he only comes back out when he's ready.

## His Moods and His Relationship With Money

Cancer men can be moody in ways that seem confusing at first, especially when he fears losing something. That guardedness often comes from deep-seated insecurity. He needs a strong sense of security and tends to be preoccupied with money, pursuing financial stability with real dedication. He'd rather save than spend, and he takes real pride in getting good value. Few people have as sharp a sense of the true worth of money as a Cancer man.

## Two Common Looks

Cancer men tend to fall into two physical types. The first has a round, soft face, a wide smile and large, almost moonlike eyes, giving him a youthful look. The second has more of the crab's features: a larger skull, a pronounced brow and cheekbones, and a strong jaw. Most Cancer men have a noticeably bony build, though some are heavier set.

## Private, Perceptive, and Loyal to Home

He keeps his own life close to the chest but is genuinely curious about everyone else's, he absorbs information and reflects it back rather than volunteering his own. He rarely moves in a straight line toward what he wants, preferring to circle it patiently until the moment is right. Once he commits, though, he doesn't easily let go.

Home matters enormously to him. No matter how much he travels, home is always his anchor, and no amount of savings ever makes him feel fully secure. He's often drawn to water, many Cancer men love the sea and some become lifelong weekend sailors.

## Health and Sensitivity

Stress and worry affect him physically more than most signs. Fear of financial trouble or losing someone he depends on emotionally can trigger real depression, which shows up physically, often in the chest, stomach, digestive system, and skin. Cancer men who lean on humor to get through hard times tend to stay healthier and live long lives. He can be vulnerable to negative thinking, but he's equally capable of remarkable self-healing once he turns toward positivity.

## His Nurturing Side

Just as he's skilled at growing his savings, he's often a genuinely gifted gardener, many Cancer men tend beautiful gardens from seed. He has a strong nurturing streak, children tug at his heartstrings, and he gives generously to causes that help kids in need. A full pantry at home represents real security to him.

The real contradiction in a Cancer man is that he can be the most romantic dreamer you know and also remarkably hard-nosed in a financial negotiation. A genuinely bad Cancer man is rare.
