**What is numerology?**

Numerology is a belief system and metaphysical practice built on the idea that numbers have mystical or divine significance and can influence a person's life. Each number carries its own energy, or vibration. By working with the numbers in your birth date and name, a numerology reading can reveal your personality, strengths, weaknesses, gifts, challenges and life purpose.

Numerology is based on the idea that numbers are a universal language, and that they hold a key to understanding our existence.

## How does numerology work?

According to numerology, each number has its own unique energy or vibration, and these vibrations can affect our lives. Numerologists use various methods, including adding up the digits of a person's birth date to calculate their "life path number," which reveals information about their personality, strengths, weaknesses and life purpose.
