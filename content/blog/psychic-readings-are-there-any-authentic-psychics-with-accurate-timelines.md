**Are there any authentic psychics who can give accurate timelines?**

Yes, sometimes, but no psychic can give you accurate dates every time. A real psychic gets what they get, and dates are some of the things they receive only SOMETIMES. Whether a date can be known depends on you and your situation at the time of the reading. If it can be known, it will be revealed; if not, it won't be, even if you call 100 psychics. If you are given an accurate timeline, it is a gift, not an entitlement.

There is so much information you can gain from [accurate psychic readings](/psychic-reading), but the most desired piece of information is the timeline, the date something will happen. In my years of giving psychic readings, I have found this is the single MOST WANTED thing people want to know. WHEN?

## Why can't a psychic always tell you when?

Some people call, and all they really want to know is when they will have what they want, need, hope for, or believe they should have. The irony is that God, the Universe, the Source, or whatever you believe in, is really in charge. Your psychic doesn't have the power to change your karma. I believe in God, and through the power of God we have the power to change the things in our lives we want to change. If you have studied spiritual principles, you may have learned that knowing the exact date you will have what you want is not in accordance with those principles.

Unfortunately, psychic networks have perpetuated this myth. When seekers call needing understanding and clarity, they ask when it will all happen, and they judge the psychic's ability by whether they hand out dates. I believe this is because imitation psychics, who dominate these services, serve up answers like, "Sure, of course, that will happen next month, or in two weeks, or tomorrow." And inevitably, the timelines move farther and farther out until you get tired of waiting or believing.

People have come to believe a psychic can guarantee a future event. This is stunningly inaccurate. Your psychic gets what they get, and dates are some of the things they get, SOMETIMES. Not every time. Even they cannot control what information they receive. Their responsibility is to tell you accurately what they are getting, and to let you know if they cannot answer your question for one reason or another.

## What helps a psychic read you clearly?

There are many factors that affect psychic readings, which is why, to get the best reading, you need to know what to expect and be relaxed and open.

For me, the easiest people to read are those with no judgments or preconceived ideas, who are open and ready to receive truthful messages. When messages flow, it's like looking through a very wide opening rather than a narrow one. A person who is closed off, intentionally or not, is harder to read. These factors affect psychic reception. Real psychics, and psychics with very high standards, will let you know what they can and cannot do, what to expect, and how to prepare for a reading.

## How do psychic messages come through?

These days, readings are like fast food. People want to drive up to the window, get their junk food and keep going, then complain later that they wish they hadn't eaten it, but go back again and again. If you stick with junk readings, you won't get to the most important messages you are meant to receive spiritually. You can get the answers you seek, though not every question will have the answer you want, and that includes dates. And you are a part of the process.

Messages come in various ways. They can come as literal audio messages, where the psychic hears information. This is my strength. They can come as visual messages, literal or symbolic, also known as clairvoyance. And some psychics are empathic, like myself, and can feel others' feelings. Your psychic must interpret the messages for you, and this skill sets a professional psychic apart from a non-professional one. Once the message has been clearly received and understood, you can use that information to guide you forward.

## Is your psychic wrong if they can't give you a date?

Not necessarily. Accurate timelines are not an entitlement. If you are given one, it is a gift, to you. Remember, your psychic is not trying to stump you. They are relaying the information they receive, interpreting it and guiding you, and if you are getting a reading from a trusted adviser, you are receiving higher guidance. This process has to be respected.

Once you start to understand what to expect in an authentic psychic reading, you stop judging your psychic. Every psychic doesn't get the same information, or get it in the same way. So judging them on what you think they should know or be ABLE to get is unfounded, and it robs you of an authentic experience based on that psychic's unique gift.

The fake psychic industry is the reason most people believe what they believe about psychics. What psychics truly offer is far from the popular perception, and real psychics suffer from this false impression. So before you accuse your psychic of not telling you something, or of getting a date wrong (assuming they are real to begin with), start from the beginning: a true understanding of spiritual readings versus garbage readings designed to entertain you. Seek out the truth from an honest spirit worker, instead of feeding off fake psychics who give real ones a bad name.

If it is so hard to find a psychic who gives accurate dates, it is reasonable to conclude that accurate timelines are specific to each person being read, depending on whether that information can be known at the time of the reading or is restricted. If it can, it will be revealed; if not, it won't be, even if you call 100 psychics. Psychics are not all-knowing superhumans. They are here to help within certain limits, set by Spirit, a powerful source of unlimited wisdom.
