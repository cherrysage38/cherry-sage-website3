**What is a real professional psychic reading?**

It is a reading from someone with genuine ability who has the training, skill, experience, knowledge and integrity to use it responsibly, and who is paid to provide a real service, not to trick you into believing they are psychic. Many people seek psychic advice without realizing that what they find are entertainment-only readings, not real professional psychic counseling. There is a big difference.

People new to psychic readings have no idea what to expect from a reading and may naively believe that if someone says they are psychic, then it must be true. That is why I am writing this article, so that new seekers as well as seasoned seekers of psychic advice can be armed with knowledge about professional versus non-professional psychic readings.

## Why is it hard to find a real psychic on a psychic network?

When you encounter a network of psychics, you run a very high risk of not finding authentic psychic advice at all. It is now well known that psychic networks are the largest purveyors of fake psychics, and going through hundreds of readers to find a real one is not only time-consuming but can be very costly.

Many sad and even angry seekers of real psychic readings have found this out the hard way. After calling hundreds of psychics on a network, they have found themselves disillusioned, disappointed and angry that they believed they were getting the truth, when in fact they were getting fabricated stories.

The reason is that there are not as many real psychics working on networks as there are fake ones. I have found that some of them are well-intentioned but really do not have the ability they wish they had, and some have simply honed their skill at giving scripted or cold readings. Cold reading is basically a skill at getting information from the client and making it seem as if the reader is telling you the information.

## How do you find a real professional psychic?

It is very difficult to find a real psychic, but not impossible. Here are some tips:

1. **Never assume you are talking to a real professional psychic on a psychic network.** The majority of them are fake. This is a plain and simple fact. Right off the bat, you are at a disadvantage, and you have to BE DETERMINED to be discerning in your selection if you choose to call psychics on a network.
2. **Take your search seriously.** If you call a bunch of network psychics to entertain yourself with their made-up details, do not think that what they are saying has any validity, or you may fall into the trap of calling way too many psychics just to verify what you've been told. You may find they say a lot of the same things, because scripted psychics all sound the same. Ever had a dozen readings that all said the same thing, yet were all wrong? This is the trap. Don't fall into it.
3. **Know who you are calling.** If you find a true professional psychic by referral or research, search for their name. On networks, readers don't use their real names, and you don't know who they are. Try to get to know the psychic counselor you are calling. Even though it may seem fun to call many psychics "just to hear what that one will say," you are not going to get what you are truly looking for by spending your time and money on fake readings. If you are lonely and just need someone to talk to, maybe this is OK.
4. **Look for a professional.** The psychic network experience can waste your time and money, so search for someone who has established themselves in providing real, professional psychic intuitive guidance, like a private psychic consultant.
5. **Stay away from gypsy psychic houses.** In these places you will find the con artist, the fraud: people preying upon vulnerable people, waiting to take as much money as they possibly can, many times draining every financial resource you have, only for you to find out in the end that they were never real and never cared about you. They are NOT spiritual people; they are thieves. If a psychic tells you that you have a curse or negativity that needs to be removed, leave immediately, or if you are on the phone, hang up. They're setting you up.

## What makes someone a professional psychic?

The psychic industry was largely set up to entertain and imitate what real psychics do. But there is real talent out there, and professional psychics doing very honest work using their gifts responsibly. Their numbers are not massive. Actually finding thousands of real psychics in one place would be amazing in itself.

Everyone has intuition, but not everyone was born to provide services that use spiritual gifts to assist people in their lives. Being a professional psychic requires training, skill, experience, knowledge and integrity. It means being paid to provide a real service, not to trick you into believing they are psychic.
