**Why do some psychic predictions turn out not to be accurate?**

Because the future is not fixed. The past and the present can be seen with a high level of accuracy, but the future develops gradually, shaped by free will and by the choices you and others make. A real psychic sees the direction your energy is flowing at the time of the reading, and that can change. Predictions also fail when they come from fake psychics, who fabricate answers and pretend to be all-knowing.

I have been providing [professional psychic readings](/psychic-reading.html) for over two decades, and I have discovered there is a great deal of confusion about the accuracy of psychic predictions. Most people would say that if a psychic makes a prediction, it's reasonable to expect it to happen. And if a psychic predicts something, we naturally assume it came from a vision or another spiritual connection, so how could it be wrong? Many people assume psychic predictions should be pretty foolproof.

The idea of seeing the future through a psychic or seer goes back centuries, as far back as Nostradamus and, in more recent history, the sleeping prophet, Edgar Cayce. With their extraordinary accuracy, we know that seeing the future is definitely possible.

## Are we supposed to know everything that will happen in the future?

Let's clarify some things about psychic predictions when you call a psychic for a reading. This is not the same scope as a psychic whose visions predict world events, or one who goes into a trance and predicts the cure for what ails you. Psychic readings today are more practical, and you should never assume you can call a psychic and learn everything that will happen to you in the future.

Some psychics predict world events. Some may see a single important event far off in your future. Some can see things that will happen in the near future. But no psychic can see with certainty every future event you may want to know about.

Let me explain why, because it's important to understand if you are going to get the most out of a real psychic reading. The past and the present can be seen with a high level of accuracy. The future is in a constant state of evolving, meaning it ***develops*** gradually. It is not fixed. Countless times I have heard, "If it's meant to be, then you should be able to tell me what will happen."

## Isn't everything already destined?

Some people believe everything is predestined. Some things in life are destined, like our parents, our family and the conditions we are born into. These conditions create the forces behind our behavior, actions and decisions, and they are meant to work out the lessons particular to a soul's growth in this lifetime.

Think of it like this. In past lives you created certain imbalances that have to be corrected in this life. This is karmic debt. We all have karma, the law of give and take, good and bad. With this in mind, live with the idea that what you create in this life will balance out at a future point. Depending on many factors, that could happen in this life or the next. But if you created an imbalance in a particular area of your life, it must be brought back into balance.

I say all of this because it is necessary to understand WHY you can't always know everything in advance. It helps to understand free will. For free will to be active in our lives, we have to be able to choose which direction we will go, and that choice has significance. If you did not have free will, your life would be strictly controlled by unseen forces, and we may as well all be robots. Free will gives us the choice to do a thing, and the consequences can reward or punish us. Our choices create our life, our destiny and our karma.

## What can a psychic predict?

A psychic reading can warn you of what's ahead and give you clues about the best choice, the possibly wrong choice, and the possible consequences. And although there are no mistakes, make no mistake: there are consequences. Your future is not a foregone conclusion.

When you ask what is going to happen, you put your psychic in the position of having to guarantee your future, as if they had the power to bring it into being rather than just see it. No psychic can guarantee your future. There can be more than one possible outcome to a situation, because YOUR action, inaction or decision is the deciding factor in creating your destiny, good or bad.

Even when prophets predict the future, there is still the possibility that we can change it. And consider this: sometimes knowing a future event could change it, because you can now manipulate the energy, which could bring about the exact opposite of what you want. And vice versa, you could change your path and choose a better course of action because of what you see coming.

## How should you ask about the future?

Most people want to know exactly what is going to happen in a relationship or their career, or whether their loved ones will find love or marry. Many, many people believe that when they call a psychic, they can find out whether someone they just met will marry them. I once had a client who wanted to know the exact date she would meet her husband, where she would meet him, the color of his hair and eyes, and even his name. I'm not kidding. This is a perfect example of the misconception people have about what a psychic can tell them about the future, and which questions can be answered accurately.

One point I want to strongly emphasize: when you request information from a spiritual source, your psychic is accessing higher consciousness, going beyond the normal mind into the infinite, unlimited part of being. Some people treat psychics like a Las Vegas slot machine: put the coin in, pull the handle, and out come the answers. You are tapping a spiritual source, and some respect is necessary. You receive information that can be known, and only that. A psychic cannot tell you things they did not receive from the Source.

## Why do predictions from fake psychics never come true?

Fake psychics fabricate their readings, and this kind of reading should be avoided. They pretend to be able to answer every one of your questions, and you ask as if they have all the answers. People who have called networks often get a false sense of accuracy because the fake psychic is "acting" all-knowing, trained to answer your questions from scripts, and you are being conditioned to believe you are getting answers from a true spiritual source. You are not.

What you've got is a warehouse of psychics who have been corporatized and streamlined for your enjoyment, managed on a large scale for profit. It's not about real readings; it's about managing so-called psychic advisers to pretend to be magical, otherworldly seers, when nothing could be further from the truth. This is why so many people have become disillusioned with psychics: the predictions never happen, because they were only pretending in the first place.

## How does a real psychic see the future?

When I'm asked what will happen, I look at the person's auric energy. What I see is what the person is channeling at the time of the reading. I can see the direction the energy is flowing, future possibilities can definitely be gleaned, and accurate predictions can be made.

Understand that what is seen is as of the time of the reading. People can change it at any time, and often do change their course of action, changing the possible outcome. Sometimes, if the energy is not flowing in the direction you want, you can see whether something can be changed to get the result you want. Sometimes the desired result can be seen, which means it was meant to be seen, or is even already set. Some things are not set, and other things have to happen first before a future event can be known.

A perfect example: if you asked a psychic, "Will I be with the one I love?" and were told yes, you might skip a necessary action that would have created that result. Then the predicted outcome doesn't happen. Why? Because you thought you had a guarantee and opted out of the learning and growing process to just wait. This is the danger of knowing too much too soon.

Use real psychics to see what is going on and to gain insight and guidance on your path, not as a soothing salve while you wait for something good to happen. A psychic is a guide, not a substitute for experiencing life or bypassing obstacles. We must all create our own destiny, and a true psychic can see what is ahead, like a lantern in the dark, tell you what you need to know, and advise you on your best course of action. None of us will ever know exactly everything that will happen in the future. There would be no point to living; we'd all just wait around for happiness to happen to us, rather than work through challenges to create a happy life.
