## What Is an Aquarius Woman Like?

The Aquarius woman is honest, independent and hard to pin down. She is loyal in love, but she keeps an emotional distance that can read as detachment, a trait common to the air signs. She has a strong individual streak: distinctive eyes, an unusual sense of style, and a wide circle of friends, though she rarely forgets her first real love.

## Aquarius Woman in Love

She is honest to a fault in relationships. The reason Aquarius women are more likely to divorce isn't a lack of commitment, it's the opposite: once a relationship becomes truly intolerable, she will walk away decisively and rarely look back. She won't rush into marriage. She needs to be completely sure of a partner first, and she will always listen closely to you, even if she doesn't follow your instructions exactly. Once she does commit, the relationship tends to be a genuinely happy one. For Aquarius women, the line between friendship and love is often blurry, many of her strongest relationships start as friendships first.

## How She Handles Trust and Money

She is curious to a fault, she will draw out everyone else's secrets, but she rarely shares her own. She keeps her real goals private while still being honest in her dealings. With money, she is careful and a little guarded, but her credit is good and she never lives beyond her means. Owing money for bad debt genuinely bothers her.

## Her Look and Personality

Aquarius women are often considered among the most striking in the zodiac, with unconventional style and an unusual sense of self-presentation. She's warm and reserved most of the time, until she surprises everyone with an opinion that has nothing to do with what anyone else in the room is saying.

## Aquarius Woman as a Mother

Motherhood doesn't come as naturally to her as it does to some signs, she likes to spread her energy across many people and interests rather than devote it all to one. But she earns her children's full trust and confidence, she's skilled at reassuring young minds and can turn tears into laughter in minutes. She won't smother her kids with affection or nag constantly, but they will always be genuinely well cared for.
