**Is your future fixed once a psychic predicts it?**

No. Apart from certain karmic events that are meant to happen, the future is not locked in. What a psychic sees about your future is based on your present energy and the free will choices you and others are about to make. If you don't like what is seen, that is your opportunity to make changes that can affect the outcome. And never just wait for a prediction as if it were guaranteed; let it go, and let it flow.

So, you want to know the future, and you are looking for someone who can give you accurate psychic predictions. Have you ever wondered whether there is more than one possible future? Do you believe your future is locked in and cannot change, so that if someone predicts it, that's it? Let's talk about the future and future psychic predictions.

## What does a psychic actually see when they look at your future?

The future is an extension of your consciousness in the present moment. I often find myself explaining possible futures and predictions. When someone asks me what is going to happen, I explain that what I see NOW about the future is based completely on your present.

You may wonder why. If you psychically see something that is going to happen, doesn't that mean it will happen? The answer is yes, sometimes. But it is comforting to know that the future is not locked in, with the exception of certain karmic events that are meant to occur. If it were, our lives would be nothing more than robotic, mindless obedience to some unseen force. And that simply is not the case. There's this thing called free will.

## How does free will shape your future?

We are all born with free will. It is the very foundation of how we evolve as humans: intelligently or unintelligently choosing our path through our decisions and choices. All of our choices define the path to our destiny, because we are creating NOW what will happen in the future. You are integral to your own destiny, because you are creating it whether you are consciously aware of it or not. Your consciousness, passive or active, is propelling you forward into your future.

So you may ask, how do I get the future I want? That involves a complex set of variables that can't be answered in a paragraph or two. But simply put, your soul's present journey is a compilation of many lifetimes that have brought you to this moment, the moment when you want to know what your future will bring. What a psychic can tell you depends on your present thoughts and the free will decisions you are about to make.

## Why shouldn't you wait for a prediction to come true?

Some people make the very serious mistake of believing that once a psychic predicts something, all they need to do is wait for it, especially if it's something they want and they are attached to the outcome. Inaction and indecision take your power away. Waiting for a prediction and watching the clock will keep it from happening. It is spiritually unsound to wait for a psychic prediction as if it were locked into a future moment you've been waiting for. It's a spiritual principle.

When a psychic predicts something for you, let it go. Let it be free to happen, as if it were your free will unleashed, because energy cannot be locked. It must flow freely.

## Can a psychic see fated events?

There are gifted psychics who can tune into some fated events in the farther-out future, and as I said, some events are fated. But in your everyday life, and in what will happen in the near future, you are dealing with free will, and if your question involves other people, their free will as well. You are dealing with the energy at this present moment, looking out into the future from here.

## Can you change what a psychic sees?

This is what I tell my clients: based on what I'm seeing in your present, this is how the future will evolve. If you don't like it, here is your opportunity to make changes that can affect the outcome. This is precisely why there are a number of possible futures: you have choices.

What choice will you make? Can you make a choice that will turn events in your favor? Yes. You cannot control another person's choice, but there is that possibility. It's wonderful. Psychic readings can help this way, by showing you where things are going and the adjustments you can make to reach your desired outcome. But never wait as if you had a guarantee.

I know we'd like to believe we can look into the future and guarantee a specific outcome. When I look into the future, I see what is going to happen based on the current energy: where the rhythm is, the frequency of the energies in question, and where they are flowing. I keep the information honest and truthful, and I will tell you what I see. I will not tell you what you want to hear, because I'd rather give you [accurate psychic predictions](/psychic-reading.html) that really happen than entertain you with fantasy.

If energy changes, yours or theirs, the future can change. The future is a moving wave of dynamic energy, which is why we are never locked into an unchangeable future unless fate is involved. So no, not all relationships are fated ones; more often they are lessons to be learned. Embrace your life with a sense of wonder and anticipation of what is to be, and don't be bogged down with regret or sorrow. Life is for learning. Seeing ahead is a gift.
