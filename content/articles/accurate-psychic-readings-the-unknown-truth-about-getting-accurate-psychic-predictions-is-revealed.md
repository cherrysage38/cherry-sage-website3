**What Defines Accurate**

That’s an easy one. Accuracy is defined as receiving accurate guidance from a higher spiritual source that is channeled through a sentient (psychic, clairvoyant, empathic) human being, who then reveals this information to you. Accuracy occurs when one is receiving information from a true psychic intuitive who has the ability to obtain (channel) this information from a source (higher consciousness) beyond themselves.

**What Factors Affect an Accurate Psychic Reading?**

Psychic accuracy can be influenced by a number of factors. First of all and basic to any reading, is that you have to be dealing with a real psychic. You will not get anywhere if you are calling a bunch of fake entertainment only psychics found on networks. These are not real psychic readings but rather a business model designed to comfort you by telling you fabricated information (mainly overly positive forecasts claiming everything will work out exactly the way you want, making you feel pretty darn good), which is delivered in the guise of coming from a special psychic ability.

There are a number of purveyors of this type of psychic reading, and unfortunately, the prevalent perception of psychics today and what they symbolize has been generated from the strong negative reactions of innumerable seekers who had the misfortune of discovering this the hard way (scripted and cold readings). So the number one factor affecting psychic accuracy is, are you really talking to a real psychic. And this is not as easy as you may think. Other factors that affect the accuracy of a reading is of course talent, experience and knowledge. Different psychic experts have varying degrees of depth to their reading and sometimes you do have to call a number of experts to find one who connects the best with your energy. You cannot assume that every psychic is using there ability in the same exact way because they probably are not. A spiritual gift that gives one the ability to see or know information from the spiritual realm connects with this realm based on their own subjective consciousness. Psychic thought is not something that can be quantified. At this time, there is no criteria that is used to define, quantify or measure the mechanics of psychic transmission, so normal definitions of reality will not apply here. So you can imagine people are born with certain sensitivities that they experience in their own unique way and whatever that is can be different for every psychic. Everyone who has a natural proclivity towards psychic awareness may not ever even develop it. But if you want accuracy, try to find a responsible psychic expert who is a professional and don’t play around with network psychics. There are some good ones on networks but they are hard to find and I’m sure they desire to distinguish themselves from the massive amounts of fake psychics out there. And they should. If you don’t trust your psychic, don’t bother calling them. Get a referral or do some research on the web by searching for real psychic readings or authentic psychic readings first. A true psychic is there to help you through your difficulties by reading your energy and those involved in your situation, then give you advice based on what is revealed.
