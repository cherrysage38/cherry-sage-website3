Set my status for today

One tap and your site changes right away. It lasts until the end of today (Eastern time), then your hours take over again. Or press Clear.

Away / vacation

Turn this on and your site shows you as away, no matter the day or time, until you turn it back off. It beats everything else, including your one-tap status.

If you pick a date, Away turns itself off after that day.

My hours

When you are online. These are the same hours your booking page offers, so the status light and the appointment scheduler always agree. Times are Eastern.

Holidays & days off

On these days your site shows you as away, and the booking page will not offer times. Add the holidays you take off, then remove any you work.
