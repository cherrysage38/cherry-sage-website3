How to bring back an earlier version

  1. Find the version you want below and click Show this version to read it.
  2. Click Copy the post text.
  3. Click Open in the editor. Click in the main text box, select all of it (Ctrl+A, or Cmd+A on a Mac) and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V).
  4. If the title or descriptions were different too, copy and paste those the same way.
  5. Read it over. When you are sure, click Publish in the editor. Until then nothing changes on your site.
