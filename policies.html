Refunds

Our refund policy

Cherry Sage makes every attempt to give everyone a great service. A reading is a service, and you are paying for Cherry's time, expertise, skill and knowledge in her field. For this reason, Cherry does not offer refunds for readings she has already completed. If, however, you have made a purchase and no longer wish to consult with Cherry, or you have not yet rescheduled your appointment, a refund will gladly be issued.
