A sacred gift

The Intuitive Connection

People always ask me when it started, and I think they're expecting some big dramatic moment. But there wasn't one, not really. I was a kid who just knew things. I knew who was on the phone before anybody picked it up, I could walk into a room and feel that something was wrong even when everyone was smiling at me, and every so often I'd hear something, clear as a bell, that turned out to be true a few days later. I thought everybody could do that. So I didn't say much about it, and for a long time I tried to ignore it, because it didn't fit into a normal life and I wanted a normal life. But it doesn't go away just because you ignore it. It gets LOUDER. And at some point I stopped fighting it and started listening, and that's how it became what I do now, helping people see what's really going on in their lives so they can make their own choices.
