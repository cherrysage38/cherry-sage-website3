Numerology · the map in your name and birth date

Numerology

Your birth date and the name you were given hold a real map. Here is how I read it, with free tools to begin.

What can numerology tell you about your life?

Numerology reads the numbers held in your full birth name and your birth date. Read together, they form a kind of map: your character, the path your life is built around, what your heart most wants, the lessons your soul came here to learn, and the timing of the seasons you move through. It is old, precise and surprisingly personal.

Welcome, dear soul. I'm so glad you've found your way here. If you've ever wondered what the numbers in your life truly mean, or you've been searching for deeper guidance on your path, you're in the right place.

What is numerology?

Numerology is the study of the numbers in your name and your birth date, and what they reveal about you. Every letter and every date carries a number. Each number has its own vibration, and each one can show up in balance, overbalanced (too much of that energy) or underbalanced (too little of it). That is why two people with the same number can look so different, and why a number is never read on its own.

In my numerology I use 11 and 22 as master numbers. They carry a higher level of their energy as well as their reduced number: 11 also works as a 2, and 22 as a 4.

How do I use numerology in a reading?

I don't just look at numbers. I feel them, sense them and align with them. My core gifts are clairvoyance, clairaudience and empathy, and numerology is one of the tools I use alongside them. The numbers show me the structure of your life; my psychic gifts show me how that structure is playing out for you right now. Together they let me give you insight that is personal to you, not a list of meanings you could look up anywhere.

What is your Life Path number?

Your Life Path number is like a map of your soul's journey. It comes from your full birth date, and it is the central number in numerology: the path, the purpose and the lessons your life is built around. Those with a Life Path 1 are often natural-born leaders, while a Life Path 4 tends to be grounded, stable and dependable. Your Life Path also shapes your relationships, your career and even your health. Try the free calculator below, then read what each Life Path number means.

What other numbers make up your numerology chart?

Your Life Path is the center, but a full chart reads many numbers together. These are the elements I read:

  • Expression: drawn from every letter of your full birth name. What you are here to build and express in this lifetime.
  • Soul Urge: drawn from the vowels in your name. What your heart most deeply wants, beneath the surface.
  • Birthday Number: the day of the month you were born. A specific gift or talent you carry with you.
  • Basic Number Meanings: the core meaning of each number, one through nine and the master numbers. The alphabet numerology speaks in.
  • Karmic Lessons: the numbers missing from your name, pointing to the lessons your soul came here to learn.
  • Karmic Debts: the numbers 13, 14, 16 and 19, carried energy asking to be balanced in this life.

Each of these is one piece of the picture, not the whole of it. Read on its own, any single number can only tell you so much.

What is the difference between a karmic debt and a karmic lesson?

A karmic lesson comes from a number missing from your name at birth. It shows an area of life that needs extra attention to balance that number's energy. A karmic debt is much heavier: a significant difficulty met in this life as payment for the misuse of energies in a past life. When 13/4, 14/5, 16/7 or 19/1 appears in your core numbers, that debt may be one of the forces shaping your life, until you accept it and do the work to balance it. A karmic lesson is much less dominating than a karmic debt.
