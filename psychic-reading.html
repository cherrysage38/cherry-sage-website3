Looking for Answers? You're Not Alone.

People seek psychic readings for all sorts of reasons. Some are curious about the future. Others are facing difficult decisions, troubled by a relationship, worried about their careers, or simply confused about something happening in their lives.

And sometimes, they just need answers.

I've been giving professional psychic readings since 1999, speaking with people from around the world about everything from everyday concerns to some extraordinarily complicated personal situations.

One thing I've learned over the years is that no two clients are exactly alike.

Some people come to me knowing precisely what they want to ask. Others are emotionally overwhelmed and have difficulty even putting their questions into words. Some are looking for clarity, while others are hoping I'll tell them that everything will turn out exactly as they want.

I understand why people feel that way. When you're emotionally involved in a situation, particularly one involving someone you love, it's not always easy to separate what you hope will happen from what may actually be happening.

That's often when people turn to a psychic.

My purpose is to help you better understand your situation through the psychic information I receive, whether we're discussing a relationship, an important decision, or what may lie ahead.

What Can a Psychic Reading Tell You?

A psychic reading can provide information about people, relationships, circumstances, and possible future developments that may not be apparent from what you already know.

Through my clairvoyant and clairaudient abilities, communication with my Spirit Guides, and Tarot when appropriate, I work to interpret information that addresses your particular questions.

But let's clear up one misconception.

Psychics aren't magicians. We don't possess unlimited magical powers, and we certainly don't know everything.

There are no real Merlins walking around with all the answers!

We're human beings with gifts that allow us to perceive information beyond ordinary observation. Those abilities can be used to help and guide people who are searching for answers.

I don't believe being psychic means knowing absolutely everything about everyone. What matters is understanding the information we receive and communicating it in a way that is useful to the person seeking guidance.

What Happens During a Psychic Reading With Me?

When you call me for a psychic reading, we begin with something very important.

Your question.

I'm interested in what you want to know.

I don't conduct general psychic readings. My readings are focused on specific questions and situations because I want to address the reason you've contacted me.

And I need a clear question.

You might be surprised how much difference the wording of a question can make. An unclear question can sometimes produce some pretty funny information!

I frequently rephrase a client's question before beginning. Because I'm communicating with my Spirit Guides, I want the question framed in a way that allows me to recognize and interpret the messages I receive.

Sometimes the information comes clairvoyantly, through visions. At other times, I receive information clairaudiently, through hearing.

When I use Tarot cards, the symbolism helps trigger those same psychic impressions. The cards are not simply objects whose meanings I memorize and recite. Their symbolism works together with the information I'm receiving.

I look for a clear meaning and an explanation of what I'm seeing or hearing.

Do You Need to Tell Me Everything?

Not necessarily.

In many situations, I need very few details.

However, when a question involves complicated circumstances, several people, or a long history of events, I may need some background to understand precisely what you're asking.

It depends on the situation.

My objective isn't to have you tell me everything so I can repeat it back to you. It's to understand your question well enough to interpret the psychic information I'm receiving.

What Questions Can You Ask During a Psychic Reading?

Over the years, I've heard just about every kind of question imaginable.

Some questions are fairly straightforward. Others involve complicated relationships, conflicting emotions, financial worries, family situations, and decisions that could change someone's life.

Love and Relationship Questions

Love and relationships are among the most common reasons people contact me.

Questions frequently include:

  • Does he or she still love me?
  • What are this person's feelings toward me?
  • Will my ex come back?
  • Will we reconcile?
  • Why has someone stopped communicating?
  • Will I hear from this person again?
  • Does this relationship have a future?
  • Will I meet someone new?

Sometimes a client is involved in a happy relationship and simply wants to understand where it's going.

Other times, the situation is much more emotionally difficult.

I've spoken with people who haven't heard from someone they love in weeks, months, or even years. Yet emotionally, that relationship remains very much alive for them.

A psychic reading can become a source of comfort, and sometimes a way of maintaining a sense of connection with someone who is no longer physically present or communicating.

I understand how powerful those feelings can be.

Some clients call for updates on previous readings. Others return with the same questions because their circumstances haven't changed, but their need for reassurance remains. (I've written more about this in Can Too Many Psychic Readings Give You the Wrong Future?)

My approach is to address what they're asking while helping them understand the situation as clearly as possible. You can read more about my love psychic readings here.

Career, Work, and Financial Questions

Not every psychic reading is about romance.

People also contact me about employment, career changes, business opportunities, workplace relationships, and financial uncertainty.

I've noticed that during economically unstable periods, calls about careers and finances tend to increase.

People worry about losing their jobs, finding new employment, making the wrong career move, or whether their financial circumstances will improve.

These are understandable concerns. When your livelihood feels uncertain, even an ordinary decision can become emotionally overwhelming.

A psychic reading can offer another perspective on the circumstances you're facing and the possibilities ahead.

Naturally, important financial and professional decisions should also take practical information into account.

Personal Decisions and Complicated Situations

Sometimes a client doesn't have one simple question.

They may be dealing with a family conflict, a difficult personal choice, or circumstances involving several people whose actions affect one another.

In these situations, I work through the questions carefully, making certain that I understand what the client wants to know.

The more complicated the situation, the more important it becomes to ask the right questions.

How I Developed My Psychic Abilities

My psychic experiences began in childhood, when I was a sensitive child who often picked up other people's strong emotions without understanding what was happening. I sometimes assumed those feelings were directed toward me. Over time, I learned to distinguish my own emotions and thoughts from what I was picking up from others, and to recognize that it wasn't always about me.

Later, while living in New York City, a numerology reading inspired me to study numerology. I went on to study Tarot and discovered that when I posed questions, I received clairvoyant visions and clairaudient information. I continued developing those abilities until I established my professional psychic reading business in 1999.

Since then, I've worked with clients worldwide. Some have continued calling for more than 20 years; my longest-standing client relationship has lasted approximately 22 years. You can read more about my psychic journey on my Meet Cherry Sage page.

What Makes My Psychic Readings Different?

I can't speak for every psychic, but I can tell you how I approach my own work.

I'm interested in giving you information that addresses your questions.

That means listening carefully, understanding what you're asking, and interpreting the psychic impressions I receive as clearly as possible.

I'm not interested in making extravagant promises or pretending to know everything.

And I won't deliberately tell you something simply because I think it's what you want to hear.

After so many years of working with people, I know how emotionally important these readings can be.

Sometimes the information is encouraging. Sometimes it raises questions you hadn't considered. And sometimes it may not be what you were hoping to hear.

My responsibility is to communicate what I genuinely receive and explain it to the best of my ability.

What About a Full Life Numerology Reading?

My Full Life Numerology Reading is a separate, much more extensive service.

Unlike my question-focused psychic readings, a Full Life Numerology Reading doesn't require you to prepare questions.

Conducted over the telephone, it is an in-depth exploration of your life through numerological calculations.

The reading examines your life from birth onward, and the forecasting tables can be calculated as far into the future as we choose.

It's a different experience from calling with a particular relationship or career question.

For people interested in exploring the broader patterns and cycles of their lives, a Full Life Numerology Reading offers a much more comprehensive approach.

Frequently Asked Questions About Psychic Readings

Do I Need to Prepare Questions Before Calling?

Yes. My psychic readings are question-focused, so I recommend thinking about what you most want to know before calling.

You don't need to phrase everything perfectly. I often help clients clarify or reword their questions during the reading.

Can You Tell Me What Someone Else Is Thinking or Feeling?

Questions about another person's thoughts, feelings, and intentions are among the most common subjects of my readings.

I use my clairvoyant and clairaudient abilities, sometimes supported by Tarot, to explore the information surrounding that person and the situation.

The important thing is to establish a clear question so I can interpret the information I receive.

Can a Psychic Tell Me Whether My Ex Will Return?

Reconciliation is one of the subjects clients frequently ask me about.

A reading can explore the other person's feelings, intentions, and the circumstances surrounding a possible reunion.

Every relationship is different, and I approach each situation individually rather than giving everyone the same answer. I've written more about this in Can a Psychic Tell If Your Ex Will Come Back?

Do You Always Use Tarot Cards?

No. I receive psychic information clairvoyantly and clairaudiently, and I use Tarot when I want to explore or clarify information through its symbolism. You can read more about my Tarot readings.

Can You Guarantee That Something Will Happen?

No psychic knows everything, and I don't offer guarantees about future events.

My work is to interpret the information I receive and share it with you as clearly and accurately as I can.

Do You Offer General Psychic Readings?

No. My regular psychic readings are focused on your specific questions.

For a more comprehensive exploration of your life without preparing individual questions, I offer Full Life Numerology Readings.

Ready to Talk About What's on Your Mind?

Whether you're struggling with a relationship, worried about a decision, wondering about your future, or simply trying to understand something that doesn't make sense, you're welcome to bring your questions to me.

You don't need to have everything figured out before you call. You just need to know what you'd like to understand.

I've been helping people explore their questions through psychic readings since 1999, and I would be pleased to help you explore yours.
