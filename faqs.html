FAQs

If your question isn’t covered below, please send me a note. Remember, I love short and succinct emails, and I will respond within 2 to 3 business days, if not sooner.

Frequently asked questions

How does a reading work?

I knew you would ask that, and that is why I have a page devoted to this question. Please see How It Works.

What kind of psychic are you?

This is a very good question to ask, because there are different types of psychic, intuitive, spiritual and mediumship readings, and not every reader has the same abilities. I am a psychic intuitive with heightened abilities as an empath, clairvoyant and clairaudient. I am also a professional numerologist. I have been practicing professionally since 1999, when cherrysage.com was born.

Do you need my date of birth? Do you connect with loved ones who have passed?

My readings do not require dates of birth. I am not a medium, and I do not connect with those who have crossed over to communicate with surviving loved ones. I am not an astrologer. Sometimes, during a reading, I will ask for the month and day someone was born. I ask occasionally to determine that person’s Personal Year vibration. This can be helpful to establish your personal energy focus for a particular year, usually the year we are in. It is in no way needed to read the energy of the people in question during a session.

How do you read me and the people in my question?

I seek answers from a higher spiritual source and connect with the energy of the people involved in your questions. I have the ability to access others’ energy fields, and this is mainly what I am connecting to during your reading, along with a higher consciousness for greater insight. I am picking up all kinds of vibrations, so it is best for you to be in a calm, relaxed state of mind and open to me, so that I can access you and others easily. If you are upset, I will not read you, as your energy will be too agitated to gain any specific insight that will be helpful.

What should I expect from my reading?

I am here to guide you, provide insight, and see the course you are on and its probable outcomes. I keep my readings reality-based, so do not expect feel-good readings. I will tell you the truth. Please be prepared for an in-depth, truthful reading. If you are not prepared for that, please do not call at this time. For more about psychic readings, you will find a wealth of information on my blog.

Is my appointment confirmed as soon as I book?

When you book through Book a Reading, you choose the time you would like for your consultation. I must have time to accept your request, so your appointment is not confirmed automatically the moment you book. I will confirm your time by email or phone after I receive your request, so please wait for your confirmation.
